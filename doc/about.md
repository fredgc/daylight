# Daylight Local Time

Have you ever said to yourself “Golly, I **hate** daylight savings time! Having
my clock jump around makes me doubt the very **continuity** of the *space-time
continuum*!” Or maybe you said it to a co-worker? Or maybe you said it to
somebody on the **bus**, after a long day at **work**? Then this app is for
you! Have you ever started driving on a **vacation** and found your clock
suddenly **jump** forward or backwards an *hour*? Disconcerting, isn’t it?
These **arbitrary** time zone changes could be a driving hazard! Well, this app
is for you!

The Daylight Local Time Zone app displays time in **your very own** Local Time
Zone. Install the app on your **Android** phone, or keep the web page open on
your **laptop**, and your clock will continuously adjust itself to the timezone
based on your current location. No more **discontinuities**!

“But, Fred, what about adjusting time so there are more hours of **daylight**
in the **winter**?” I hear you say, quite clearly, as if you are standing right
behind my **left** shoulder. This app solves that problem, too! In the daylight
local time zone, time is adjusted so that 6:00 AM is **sunrise** and 6:00 PM is
**sunset**. At first glance, this seems impossible, but using an advanced
interpolation algorithm, we can adjust the number of seconds in each minute so
that there are **exactly** 12 hours of daylight **EVERY** day.

Daylight Local Time is **personalized**, because we use state of the art
*Machine Learning Technology* to “learn” YOUR current location. This location
is projected onto an **ellipsoid** and fed into formulae devised by
**international astrophysicists** and used by **NASA**’s Global Positioning
Satellite System. From these formulae we can compute the **exact** time of
sunrise and sunset.

**Internationalization:** This app supports internationalization because the
clock uses 24 hour time. If you are not a foreigner, this means the time is
normal in the morning, but in the afternoon you should **subtract 12** from the
time.

**Privacy:** This app uses location information to personalize your time zone,
but it does not store on a *server* or in the **cloud** this location
information or any other **personal information**, such as sex, weight, height,
tooth-to-tattoo ratio or age. If it did, we would delete it right away and not
look at it.

**Accessibility:** This app uses a big font for the clock, so it should be
**OK**.

**Known Problems:** This app uses a standard ellipsoidal model for the
**Earth**, so sunrise and sunset might be a little off if you are a coal
**miner** or climbing a **big** mountain or **flying** in a plane or something.
Also, it is for Earth only, and only runs on **Android** and the **Web**. If
you have an iPhone or a *Blackberry* phone, or you live on another planet, like
**Pluto**, then this app won’t work. If you are from another planet, but
visiting **Earth**, it should work. Welcome! Have a fun time, but please don’t
mutilate any cows.

**Unknown Problems:** The beta testers found some problems, but I lost the
piece of paper that I wrote them on, so they aren’t really known anymore. If
you find any problems, let me know.
