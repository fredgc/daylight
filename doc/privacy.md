# Privacy Policy for Daylight Local Time

*Last Updated: October 31, 2026*

---

## Zero-PII & Privacy by Design

The **Daylight Local Time** ("the App") is an interactive educational clock
application that displays time based on solar noon and the natural position of
the Sun. We adhere to strict **Privacy by Design**: we do not collect personal
information, we do not require account registration, we do not track you
across websites, and we do not sell or monetize user data.

This application is built for students, teachers, researchers, and astronomy
enthusiasts of all ages.

---

## Information We Do NOT Collect

To maintain a zero-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) architecture:

* **No User Accounts:** You can use the clock without creating an account or
  logging in.

* **No Server Storage:** We do not store your location, settings, or usage
  data on external servers.

* **No Advertising or Cross-Site Tracking:** The App is completely ad-free and
  contains no advertising networks, marketing trackers, or commercial
  analytics SDKs.

---

## Data Handled by the Application

### 1. Device Location Data

* The App optionally requests access to your device's coarse or fine
  geographic location (latitude and longitude) solely to compute the Sun's
  position, solar noon, and Daylight Local Time for your immediate position.

* **100% On-Device Processing:** Location coordinates are processed
  exclusively in real time on your device and are **never** transmitted to,
  logged by, or shared with `gchouse.org` or any third party.

* You may alternatively enter coordinates manually or deny location permission
  without disabling the clock.

### 2. Local Settings & Preferences

* UI preferences (such as light or dark theme and display toggles) are stored
  locally on your device via `SharedPreferences` (on Android) or
  `localStorage` (on Web).

* Clearing your browser cache or uninstalling the app permanently removes this
  data.

---

## Third-Party Infrastructure

The App is hosted statically using **Google Firebase Hosting** (Google LLC):

* Standard technical connection metadata (such as IP addresses and HTTP
  request headers) is processed temporarily by Google Cloud infrastructure
  strictly for routing, security, and denial-of-service prevention.

* For more information on Google's privacy practices, see the
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) .

---

## Children & Classroom Safe (COPPA & FERPA)

The App is designed to be fully compliant with educational privacy
regulations:

* **COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act):** The App does not
  collect personal information from children under 13. Location data is not
  transmitted or stored off-device.

* **FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act):** Because no student
  education records or personal identifiers are collected, maintained, or
  shared, the App can be safely used in K-12 and university classroom
  environments.
