## 3. Solar Calculation & Geographic Approximations

All displayed solar hours, solar angles, and daylight calculations rely upon
mathematical approximations of orbital mechanics, solar declination, the
equation of time, and standard atmospheric refraction models:

* Natural variations in local topography, elevation, weather, atmospheric
  conditions, and horizon obstructions will cause actual observable sunrise
  and sunset times to vary from simulated values.

* The authors make no representations or guarantees regarding the astronomical
